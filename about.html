Our Story

About OrigamiForAll

Origami originated in China and was popularized in Japan. The word itself comes from the Japanese ori (to fold) and kami (paper). For centuries it has been a hobby, a meditation, a teaching tool and a quiet act of beauty.

It is incredible to watch an ordinary piece of paper transforming into a three-dimensional form. The more complex the design, the more incredible this process becomes — and yet some of the most loved models, like the crane and the boat, are also among the simplest.

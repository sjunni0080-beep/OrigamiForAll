THE GENTLE ART

From a single sheet, a whole world.

Origami originated in China and was popularized in Japan. It transforms paper into three-dimensional forms — without scissors, without glue, just folds. Whether you are five or eighty-five, there is a model here for you. Our community shares thousands of creations, and this gallery celebrates the beauty of paper folding around the globe.
